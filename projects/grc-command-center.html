Executive Summary

A prototype that connects applications, risks, controls, evidence, assessments, findings and remediation, so an auditor or control owner can follow one thread from end to end.

It applies GRC-as-Code concepts: controls are structured records with owners and framework links, not rows in unconnected spreadsheets.

Business Context

Overlapping frameworks tend to create duplicated control and evidence work. Linking the records improves traceability and lays the groundwork for evidence automation.

Scope

The prototype models the records below and the relationships between them.

  • AssetsApplications and systems in scope
  • RisksLinked to the assets they affect
  • ControlsLinked to risks, each with an owner
  • FrameworksRequirements mapped to controls
  • EvidenceShowing that a control operates
  • AssessmentsTesting controls against evidence
  • FindingsRaised from assessments
  • Corrective actionsTracking findings to closure
  • Control ownershipAccountability for each control

Environment

A sanitized portfolio environment. The roadmap covers automated AWS evidence collection from these services:

  • IAM
  • CloudTrail
  • AWS Config
  • Security Hub
