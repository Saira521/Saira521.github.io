Building an ISMS for a private healthcare provider

A structured, risk-based ISMS to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of patient and organisational information at Medivanta Health Ltd.

Fictional organisation. Medivanta Health Ltd. was created for educational, professional development and portfolio purposes. All details are illustrative and do not represent an actual healthcare provider or client engagement.

The organisation

A London-based provider combining private clinics with digital health services, and handling sensitive patient data at every step.

IndustryHealthcare and digital health
HeadquartersLondon, United Kingdom
PeopleAbout 75 personnel
Facilities2 private clinics
PatientsAbout 5,000 registered
Key systemsEHR, cloud-hosted applications, Microsoft 365, appointment booking, IAM, security monitoring and logging, backup and disaster recovery
Third partiesLaboratories, diagnostic providers, technology vendors and other healthcare partners

What is at stake

Key information assets

  • Patient medical records and clinical histories
  • Personally identifiable information
  • Appointment and consultation records
  • Employee and healthcare professional records
  • Financial and billing information
  • Authentication credentials and access records

Threats the ISMS must address

  • Unauthorised disclosure of patient information
  • Ransomware attacks
  • System disruptions
  • Compromised user accounts
  • Third-party security failures
