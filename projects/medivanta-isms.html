Executive Summary

A structured, risk-based ISMS to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of patient and organisational information.

Medivanta Health is a London-based private healthcare provider combining clinical services with digital health platforms. This case study builds its ISMS from context and scope through to internal audit, management review and certification readiness.

~75employees
2private clinics
~5,000registered patients

Business Context

Medivanta delivers outpatient consultations and specialist appointments, patient administration, online booking and patient communication, and works with external laboratories and diagnostic providers. Sensitive information moves through all of these services.

A security failure could affect patient confidentiality, business continuity, regulatory compliance and the organisation's reputation.

Scope

The ISMS covers both clinics, the head office and the digital platforms. Laboratories and technology vendors stay outside the boundary and are managed as suppliers. The draft scope statement sets out the boundaries and interfaces.

Key information assets

  • Patient medical records and clinical histories
  • Personally identifiable information
  • Appointment and consultation records
  • Employee and healthcare professional records
  • Financial and billing information
  • Authentication credentials and access records
  • Clinical and operational documentation

Methodology

The work follows the ISMS lifecycle, using the clause structure of ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

  1. PlanClauses 4 to 7: context, leadership, planning, support
  2. DoClause 8: operation and risk treatment
  3. CheckClause 9: monitoring, internal audit, management review
  4. ActClause 10: nonconformity and continual improvement
