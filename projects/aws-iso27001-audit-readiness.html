Executive Summary

All five assessed risks remain High after controls, and three findings are open, because configured controls could not yet prove they operate over time.

The package separates control design, implementation and operating effectiveness. Residual risk stays elevated until evidence supports reducing it.

Business Context

AsterCloud Learning Ltd. provides a multi-tenant learning platform to universities and professional training organisations, and is preparing for an ISO/IEC 27001 audit. It handles customer contact details, learner profiles, authentication metadata, course activity and support tickets.

Business priorities

  • Protect customer and learner information.
  • Maintain platform availability.
  • Detect unauthorised activity quickly.
  • Demonstrate consistent control operation to customers and auditors.
  • Reduce manual evidence collection through automation.

Scope

The design, development, operation, monitoring and support of the AsterCloud SaaS platform.

In scope

  • AWS production accounts
  • Shared security and logging services
  • EKS, RDS and S3
  • IAM and workforce access
  • GitHub CI/CD for production
  • Change and incident management
  • Backup and recovery
  • Vulnerability management and access reviews

Initially out of scope

  • Employee personal devices, unless they provide privileged administrative access
  • Customer-owned endpoints
  • Third-party systems that do not process or protect scoped information

Methodology

  1. Scope
  2. Assets
  3. Risks
  4. Controls
  5. Testing
  6. Findings
  • Likelihood and impact are scored from 1 to 5. Inherent risk is assessed before existing controls are considered.
  • Control effectiveness is rated separately from 0 (not implemented) to 4 (strong and evidenced over time), using design, implementation and operating evidence.
  • Residual risk is reassessed only against verified control operation, not because a policy exists or a tool is enabled.

Lessons Learned

The project is in progress, so these are the working principles so far.

  • Show operating evidence, not just policy documents.
  • Separate control design, implementation and operating effectiveness.
  • Keep inherent and residual risk distinct.
  • Use structured, machine-readable controls where possible.
